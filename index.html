Everything in one place

Built for a fleet, not adapted to one

Group your accounts, price them, trade between them and work the Market, without signing into them one at a time.

Multi-account management

Organise accounts into environments and folders. Read one account, roll up an environment, or take a global total across the whole fleet.

Inventory and trade locks

Every item with its market value and the exact date its trade lock lifts. Storage units are read too, so nothing hides inside a casket.

Mass trading

Move items between your accounts or out to any trade link, singly or in bulk. Balances and locks are checked before anything sends, and every offer is confirmed by 2FA.

Trade offer manager

Every incoming and outgoing offer across all accounts on one screen, each side priced. Accept, decline or cancel in batches.

Market buying

Place buy orders from the dashboard. Search an item, pull its live price, and SSIM places and confirms the order. It rests until it fills.

Market selling

List at the lowest price, an undercut, or a fixed net payout. You see exactly what lands in the wallet before anything goes live.

Order tracking

Every open buy and sell order across the fleet in one list, cancellable in place or in bulk. Mass buys force-check balances before they run.

Portfolio value and history

Every refresh snapshots item worth and wallet balance, so the curve tracks real holdings per environment and across everything.

Trade-up contracts

Build and run trade-up contracts from inventory you already hold, without leaving the dashboard.

Per-account proxies

Assign an HTTP or SOCKS proxy per account or per environment. A built-in checker reports each one's exit IP, country and latency.

Encrypted credential vault

Bulk import your accounts once. Passwords and 2FA secrets are held under a master password you set, and stay on your disk.

Native wallet currency

Each account is priced and formatted in its own Steam wallet currency. No conversions, no hard-coded euros or dollars.

Security

Your credentials stay on your machine.

The vault's strength comes from your password and from standard, reviewable cryptography, not from the code being secret. That is why publishing the source costs you nothing.

Runs on your machine

Passwords and 2FA secrets go nowhere except to Steam itself, at login, over TLS. No server of this project is involved in day to day use.

Localhost by default

The dashboard binds to loopback, with a DNS-rebinding guard and same-origin checks in front of every API route. Exposing it to your network is an explicit opt-in.

Encrypted at rest

The credential vault uses scrypt key derivation and AES-256-GCM, keyed from the master password you set. A copied folder is useless without it.

2FA handled on-device

Trades and listings are confirmed with your identity_secret locally. Your 2FA keys are only ever used on your own PC.

No telemetry, no accounts

SSIM does not report usage and requires no registration. Its only outbound connections are Steam, CSFloat if you enable it, and GitHub for update checks.

You can read the code

About 31,000 lines of TypeScript across 99 files, with an architecture overview, documented invariants and 293 test files.

Verified releases

Builds are cut by GitHub Actions and published with a SHA256SUMS file and a provenance attestation, so you can prove the binary came from this repository.

Verified updates

The updater checks a SHA-256 hash and an Ed25519 signature before replacing the running program, and rejects anything that does not match.

Install

Download it and open it.

One file, no installer, no sign-up. SSIM is a single self-contained executable with its backend built in.

1

Download

Get SSIM.exe from the Releases page. Around 500 MB of free disk space is enough.

2

Verify

Check the file's hash against the SHA256SUMS published with the same release. Commands below.

3

Give it a folder

It creates data, Vault, logs and mafiles beside itself, so do not run it from Downloads.

4

Set a master password

On first launch it asks for one, and that password encrypts your vault. There is no recovery for it. Keep it somewhere safe.

Check the hashPowerShell
Get-FileHash .\SSIM.exe -Algorithm SHA256
Compare the result with SHA256SUMS from the same release. If they differ, do not run the file, and please tell us.
Check the provenanceGitHub CLI
gh attestation verify SSIM.exe -R Santerxyz/SSIM
Confirms the binary was built by GitHub Actions from this repository, and not repackaged by someone else.

Download SSIM only from the Releases page

SSIM handles Steam passwords and 2FA secrets. A copy from a forum, a Discord message, or any site advertising an unlocked build is not worth the risk. The only official source is github.com/Santerxyz/SSIM/releases.

Before you start

Worth knowing

SSIM automates actions on Steam accounts, and Steam's Subscriber Agreement restricts automated access. Use it on accounts you own, and decide for yourself whether that trade-off suits you.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it really free?+

Yes. SSIM is free software under the Apache License 2.0. You can use it, read it, change it, redistribute it and fork it. There is no paid tier and nothing is held back.

Do I need an account, a licence key or an invite?+

No. There are no accounts, no licence keys, no activation and no closed beta. Download the executable from the Releases page and open it.

What do I need to get started?+

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, around 500 MB of free disk space, and the Steam accounts you control. Adding an account's 2FA secret lets SSIM confirm trades and listings for you.

Where is my data stored?+

On your PC, in folders SSIM creates next to the executable. Passwords and 2FA secrets live in an encrypted vault, using scrypt key derivation and AES-256-GCM keyed from your master password. They are sent nowhere except to Steam itself, at login, over TLS.

How do I know the download is genuine?+

Every release ships a SHA256SUMS file. Run Get-FileHash .\SSIM.exe -Algorithm SHA256 and compare. Releases are also built by GitHub Actions and published with a provenance attestation, which you can check with gh attestation verify SSIM.exe -R Santerxyz/SSIM.

How do updates work?+

SSIM checks GitHub for a newer release and can install it for you. The updater verifies a SHA-256 hash and an Ed25519 signature before replacing the running program, and rejects anything that does not match.

Does Steam allow this?+

Steam's Subscriber Agreement restricts automated access to the service, and SSIM automates actions on Steam accounts. It talks to Steam through the same public web endpoints your browser uses. Use it on accounts you own, and make your own call on whether that trade-off suits you.

Can I use proxies?+

Yes, HTTP or SOCKS, set per account or per environment. Each account uses its own connection, and a built-in checker reports the exit IP, country and latency for each one.

Can I contribute, or fork it?+

Both. CONTRIBUTING.md points at the architecture, the invariants worth knowing before touching trading code, and issues labelled good first issue. Building takes two commands and needs no keys.

SSIM is maintained in spare time, so issues and pull requests are read but not always quickly. Forks are welcome. The name and logo are not covered by the licence, so give your fork its own name.
