Security & trust

Your credentials stay on your PC.

SSIM is built local-first. Here is exactly how your accounts are protected.

Runs on your machine

Passwords and .maFile secrets never get uploaded. The only thing that leaves the PC is one signed license check.

Localhost only

The dashboard binds to 127.0.0.1 and is never exposed to your network, with DNS-rebinding protection built in.

Isolated sessions

Each account uses its own connection and proxy. Sessions never mix, so one can't affect another.

2FA handled on-device

Trades and listings are confirmed with your identity_secret locally. Your 2FA keys are only ever used on your PC.

Encrypted at rest

Set a master password and SSIM encrypts your stored data with AES-256-GCM. A copied folder is worthless without the key.

Hardware-locked license

Licenses are Ed25519-signed and bound to your device's hardware ID. A key can't be copied to another machine or forged.

Tamper-resistant build

The app ships as compiled V8 bytecode with an obfuscated licensing layer, so the source isn't sitting in the binary.

Signed updates

Every auto-update is checked by SHA-256 hash and Ed25519 signature before it runs, over HTTPS. Only genuine builds install.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it free?+
Yes, SSIM is free during the closed beta. No payment and no card. Join the Discord or message Santer.xyz for an invite.
How do I get access?+
Access is invite-only for now. Join the Discord at discord.gg/mTrb6mGUyK or message Santer.xyz to request a spot and your license key.
Where is my data stored?+
On your PC, in the folder next to the app. Account passwords and .maFile secrets are never uploaded. Set a master password and that data is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
What do I need to get started?+
Your accounts' .maFile files (Steam Desktop Authenticator format) and, for bulk import, an optional accounts.txt with user:pass lines. Drop them in the folder and import.
Does it support both CS2 and TF2?+
Yes. Switch between CS2 (inventory, trading, market) and TF2 (read-only inventory with key counting) with one toggle in the stat bar.
Can I manage trade offers across accounts?+
Yes. The trade-offer manager pulls every incoming and outgoing offer from all your accounts into one list, prices each side, and lets you accept, decline or cancel in batches.
How do updates work?+
SSIM updates itself. On launch it checks for a newer signed build, verifies it by SHA-256 and Ed25519 signature, and installs it before starting. You never download anything by hand.
Can I use proxies?+
Yes, HTTP or SOCKS, set per environment or per account. Each account uses its own connection, and a built-in checker shows its exit IP, country and latency.
